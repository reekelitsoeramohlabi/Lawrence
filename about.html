The heart of the highlands

A kingdom with room to breathe.

Meet a mountain country shaped by its people, its altitude and its generous spirit.

A country above the clouds

Small in size.Immense in feeling.

Lesotho is a high-altitude kingdom surrounded by South Africa, where dramatic escarpments rise above green valleys and villages. Its people, the Basotho, share a deep connection to the land, a rich cultural heritage and a tradition of welcoming visitors.

From the bustle of Maseru to remote mountain passes, every journey brings a different perspective on this remarkable place.
